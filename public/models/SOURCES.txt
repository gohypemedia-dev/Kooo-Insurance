3D models in this folder come from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com). They are licensed CC0 (public domain), so no attribution is required.
Source: the 1k glTF downloads. Processed with gltf-transform 4.5.1: welded, deduplicated and pruned, with foliage thinned and meshes simplified (meshoptimizer), textures converted to WebP, and geometry compressed with EXT_meshopt_compression and KHR_mesh_quantization.
Loading in three.js: GLTFLoader needs loader.setMeshoptDecoder(MeshoptDecoder) from three/examples/jsm/libs/meshopt_decoder.module.js.
